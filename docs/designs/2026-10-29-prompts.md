# Prompts: matches without a trigger

Long prompts for AI tools were kept as matches, as `;brain` in `ai.yml`: found
only by a trigger to remember and the first line, edited in a small field.
Sokki now keeps them as matches without a trigger, found and inserted from the
palette, and makes one from the text selected in any app.

## Model

A match's `trigger` is optional. A match without one is a prompt: the engine
leaves it out of the trie (`engine.NewMatcher`), and the palette inserts it,
pasted when it is longer than `clipboard_threshold`. A prompt may still have a
trigger; there is no kind of match apart.

```yaml
matches:
  - label: Review a diff
    replace: |
      Review this diff for bugs. Be brief.
```

A match without a trigger, label or replacement fails to load. `MarshalYAML`
leaves `trigger` out when it is empty.

## Identity

Matches were known by their trigger everywhere: the IDs kept across reloads,
`recency.json`, `usage.json`, the sync merge and duplicates. They are now known
by `Match.Key()`: the trigger, or for a prompt its `Title()` (the label, else
the first line with text). A match with a trigger keeps its trigger as its key,
so the files written before need no migration. Renaming a prompt's label is,
as renaming a trigger was, a new match to recency, usage and sync.

`Store.WithKey` finds a match by key; the loader keeps one match of a key, as
it did of a trigger, and warns of the other.

## Palette

`palette.go` keeps the window and three modes; `palette_search.go` ranks;
`palette_add.go` has the form and capture.

- **Search**: as before, with rows of a prompt showing a "Palette" plate where
  the keycap was (`triggerCap`), `⌘C` to copy the chosen match with its
  variables filled in (then said as Saved says, for 1.5 s), and No Results
  offering `⌘N`.
- **New Prompt (`⌘N`)**: a form inside the palette. Its text is the selection
  in the app in front (`macos.SelectedText`), else the clipboard snapshot of
  the palette's opening, with a caption saying which. What was searched is its
  label, or its trigger when it looks like one (one word starting with
  punctuation). It saves to `prompts.yml` with `⌘↵`, refusing a key another
  match has, and goes back to the search with the prompt chosen, marked New,
  and the footer saying where it went, for 2.5 s. `⌘E` carries
  it to the editor (`editorWindow.compose`); `esc` goes back.
- **Saved**: the capture shortcut (`capture_shortcut`: none, ⌃⌥P, ⌃⌥S or ⌃⇧P)
  and **New Prompt from Selection** in the menu save the selection at once,
  labeled by its first line (60 characters, numbered when taken). The palette
  then shows, 64 high and without taking the keyboard (`ShowInactive`), what
  was saved with Undo and Edit…, or "Nothing selected", and hides after 3 s
  unless the pointer is over it, a line at its foot running out meanwhile.
  The shortcut does not fall back to the clipboard, which would save
  something old without a word.

Each showing of the palette comes in (160 ms) and each mode slides into it
(180 ms); the choice, the preview and the search change at once, so no
motion stands between a key and its result. A query that extends the last
searches only what that one found, and a mask of each match's runes skips
most matches before the fuzzy scan.

## Outside the palette

- **Matches** has a scope bar, All, Triggers or Prompts (`matchList.kind`);
  New and `⌘N` make a prompt while it shows prompts, in `prompts.yml` unless
  a file is shown.
- **The editor** calls what it edits a prompt while the trigger is empty
  ("New Prompt", "Edit Prompt"), with the prompt icon, and says under the
  fields how it shows in the palette and the palette's shortcut. A trigger
  typed turns it into a match again; the title and icon follow at once.
- **The menu** shows the palette and capture shortcuts as key equivalents
  (`accelerator`), and dims New Prompt from Selection without Accessibility.

The palette, editor, Matches and Insights share `style.go`'s motion
(`motionFast` 120 ms for hover, `motionBase` 180 ms for what comes in,
`motionGrow` 240 ms for bars), rows (`rowFill`, `rowColors`), heads
(`iconPlate`, `heading`) and empty states.

## Selection

`macos.SelectedText` asks the frontmost app's focused element for
`AXSelectedText` through Accessibility, with a 0.3 s timeout. It reads nothing
during secure input, nothing of Sokki itself, and logs nothing; it does not
synthesize ⌘C, which would change the clipboard. Apps that don't report a
selection (some Electron apps) fall back to the clipboard in the form.

## Compatibility

A Sokki before this one fails a match file with a match without a trigger, as
a whole. Prompts made here go to `prompts.yml`, so an older Sokki reading the
same folder loses only that file. Sync ships in the same release.
